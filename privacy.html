Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HeroVerse Channel Operations, operated by HeroVerse Operations under the authorized channel operator's supervision, accesses and uses Google user data.

Data accessed

After explicit OAuth consent, the application may access the authorized YouTube channel ID, title, handle, public channel/video metadata, aggregate YouTube Analytics results, and the permissions needed to upload approved videos, thumbnails and caption tracks and finalize their public visibility. Google exposes caption upload and visibility update through the broader youtube.force-ssl scope; application policy limits its use to captions.insert and videos.update for status.privacyStatus. It does not request Gmail, contacts, payment or Google Ads data.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to verify channel identity, prepare and validate publication packages, stage approved video privately, attach thumbnail and caption content, verify processing, finalize public visibility, monitor owned-channel performance, detect operational problems and improve future editorial decisions for that same authorized operation. This integration does not use the broad scope to delete videos, ratings, comments or captions or to change unrelated metadata.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials are kept outside the project workspace in a location controlled by the operating system user. Secrets are not written to project reports or source files. Operational evidence stores identifiers, status, aggregate measurements and hashes only when required for auditability. No security method can eliminate all risk.

Sharing and sale

Google user data is not sold. It is not shared for advertising. Data is transmitted to Google APIs as required to perform the authorized functions. A service provider may be used only when necessary, authorized, disclosed, contractually compatible and configured under the project’s privacy and least-access controls.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained only while the owner keeps the integration active. The owner can revoke access from Google Account security settings and can request deletion of locally retained credentials and operational data by contacting willmarquesfotografia@gmail.com. Audit records that must be retained for security, rights or legal obligations will be limited to what is necessary and documented.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

TikTok data

After the owner authorizes the HeroVerse TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the application uses the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish only to read that account's basic profile (to confirm the account and its allowed privacy options) and to post the same approved Shorts already published on the HeroVerse YouTube channel, labelled as AI-generated content. It does not read followers, messages, comments or other users' data. TikTok tokens are kept only in encrypted repository secrets, are never written to files or logs, and are not sold or shared. The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions).

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when data practices or scopes change. Questions and deletion requests: willmarquesfotografia@gmail.com.
